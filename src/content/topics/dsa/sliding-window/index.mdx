## Recognise it

- The answer is a **contiguous** subarray or substring.
- You want the longest/shortest window satisfying a condition, or the best value over all windows of size k.
- The condition is **monotone**: if a window is invalid, every larger window containing it is also invalid (e.g. "has a repeated character", "sum > k" with positive numbers).

<Callout type="analogy">
A train window passing a landscape: as the train moves, one tree enters on the right and one leaves on the left. You don't re-look at the whole view; you only update for what changed.
</Callout>

### Longest substring without repeating characters

<Pitfalls>

- **Shrinking with `if` instead of `while`** — the window may need several steps to become valid.
- **Recording the answer at the wrong moment** — for "longest", after shrinking to valid; for "shortest", while valid during shrinking.
- **Non-monotone conditions** (sums with negatives, "exactly k") — use prefix sums, or count "at most k" minus "at most k−1".
- **Recomputing window state from scratch** each step — O(n·k).

</Pitfalls>

<KeyTakeaways>

- Expand right, shrink left while invalid, update the answer — O(n).
- Maintain counts so validity checks are O(1).
- Monotonic deques give window maxima/minima in O(n).

</KeyTakeaways>
